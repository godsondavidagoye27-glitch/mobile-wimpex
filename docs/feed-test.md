# Feed and post test checklist

- Create two accounts and verify follow and unfollow update the Following feed.
- Set an account to private and verify its posts are hidden until a follow is accepted.
- Block an account and verify neither account can see the other's posts.
- Add a comment and a reply, then verify deleting a comment behaves correctly.
- Delete a post and verify it disappears from the feed and storage.
- Upload a video over a slow network and confirm progress is visible.
- Close the app during an upload, reopen it, and confirm the upload resumes.
- Try to select a 61 second video and confirm it is rejected.
- Try to select a video over 50 MB and confirm it is rejected.
- Enable airplane mode and verify the offline message appears while cached feed items remain visible.
